I'm excited to share my experience at **SeleniumConf 2026**! This was an amazing event filled with insightful talks, great networking opportunities, and valuable interactions with the testing and automation community.

## The Conference

SeleniumConf 2026 brought together passionate engineers, QA professionals, and developers from around the world. The energy was electric, and there was a strong focus on the latest trends in test automation and quality engineering.

## My Talk: Quality on Autopilot: Scaling Testing in Uyuni

I had the privilege to present **"Quality on Autopilot: Scaling Testing in Uyuni"**, where I shared our journey of adopting TestOps practices to bring automation, visibility, and resilience into our quality engineering process at Uyuni.

### Talk Overview

The presentation covered how we scale quality in a fast-moving open source project by implementing several key practices:

**Infrastructure & Environment Automation**
- Using OpenTofu and Salt to lay the foundation for consistent, on-demand testing environments
- Designing custom DevContainers to improve engineer experience on their IDEs

**Test Observability & Analytics**
- Implementing Prometheus and Grafana to analyze historical trends and gain visibility into test behavior
- Tracking flaky tests and known issues through a custom system that connects QE workflows with test reports

**Smart Testing & Feedback Loops**
- Smart test selection in Pull Requests to target only affected components with relevant end-to-end tests
- Integrated synthetic monitoring of key product metrics into daily pipelines for continuous health insights

### Key Takeaway

Our journey demonstrates that small, iterative changes in quality engineering can help align quality with development at scale—a critical aspect of maintaining a healthy open-source project while moving fast.

The talk was well-received, and I appreciate all the great questions and discussions afterward. It's always fulfilling to share knowledge with the community and learn from their experiences and perspectives.
