Professional Summary

QE Architect and software engineer with 20 years of experience in Linux, test automation and CI/CD, now moving into Platform and Infrastructure Engineering. At SUSE I built and validated Linux environments at scale: infrastructure as code with Terraform/OpenTofu, Salt and Ansible, RPM and image builds with OBS, KIWI and Cobbler, zero-touch provisioning (PXE/TFTP, AutoYaST, cloud-init, Combustion), Jenkins pipelines, and Prometheus/Grafana observability.

Earlier, at Telefónica, I tested the components and the performance of a national CDN and benchmarked it against Akamai. I work mostly in Python, Bash and Go, and I share what I learn through open source, conference talks and mentoring.
